The Firm

We argue cases in court and settle them out of it.

From our chamber on Sat Mosjid Road in Dhanmondi, we act for individuals, businesses, banks and institutions across the country. Litigation, corporate advisory, constitutional matters and land disputes stay with the same team, so the lawyer who hears your case at the first meeting is the one who argues it.

Contentious matters

Civil, criminal, writ and appellate advocacy before the Supreme Court and subordinate courts.

Non-contentious work

Structuring, compliance, contracts, due diligence and regulatory filings for companies.

Direct access

You speak to the lawyer running your file. The chamber is open 9:00 am to 10:00 pm.
